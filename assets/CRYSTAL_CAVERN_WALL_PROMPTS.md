## Color Palette Reference

- **Stone**: Dark charcoal (#2d2d2d), deep gray (#1f1f1f)
- **Crystals**: Purple (#8b5cf6), cyan (#06b6d4)
- **Crystal Glow**: Bright cyan (#6efcff)
- **Shadows**: Near black (#0a0a0a)

---

## Usage Notes

- **Tileable textures**: Edges must seamlessly connect for repeating patterns
- **Decorative elements**: Non-tileable, placed strategically
- **Transparency**: All assets need transparent backgrounds
- **Style**: Consistent dark cave aesthetic with purple-cyan crystal accents
- **Side-view**: All assets viewed from 2D side perspective

These wall assets will make tunnels and rooms visually enclosed and atmospheric!
